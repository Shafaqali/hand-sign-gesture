What's real here: live MediaPipe hand-landmark tracking (21 points/hand, both hands) feeding two independent rule-based geometric classifiers. Word signs mode recognizes 13 whole-word signs — including two-hand compounds (Help, Together) and one motion-tracked sign (Bye, via wrist-trajectory wave detection) — and turns a run of them into a punctuated sentence through a phrase-template engine. Alphabet mode fingerspells 16 ASL letters (A, B, C, D, F, I, J, L, O, S, U, V, W, X, Y, Z) from static handshapes plus two motion-traced letters (J, Z), with a hold-time + rolling-buffer confirmation layer so a shape has to be held steadily before it registers, auto-spacing between words on a short pause, and line finalization into the Conversation log on a longer pause. Both modes share Web Speech API TTS, detection history, CSV export, and a fixed-vocabulary translation table.

What's simplified: both modes are geometry-over-landmarks classifiers, not trained models — they recognize curated, clearly distinct handshapes rather than general ASL/ISL. Alphabet mode only covers 16 of the 26 letters; E, G, H, K, M, N, P, Q, R, and T are left out because they depend on fine thumb/finger overlap or wrist rotation that's unreliable to detect from 2D landmarks with simple geometric rules. J and Z rely on a coarse fingertip-trajectory heuristic (a handful of direction reversals in a short time window), not real motion-sequence modelling, so they'll be the least reliable letters. The "natural sentence" text in Word signs mode is a hand-written phrase table over 13 known signs, not grammar inference. Reliable, general sign-language recognition — the full alphabet, facial-grammar markers, body posture, and regional dialects — needs a trained deep-learning model on a labeled landmark dataset, which isn't something that can be fabricated without real training data. A production version would swap both classifiers for that trained model and add the FastAPI + MongoDB + JWT backend for accounts, cross-device history, and the admin panel — happy to scaffold that backend next if useful.
