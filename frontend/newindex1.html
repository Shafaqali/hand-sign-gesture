● About this build

About MR Voxa

What's real, what's simplified, and how the pieces fit together.

MR Voxa turns hand signs into spoken, written words — live, in the browser.

It tracks 21 hand landmarks per hand with MediaPipe, classifies the handshape with geometric rules (or a model you supply), assembles recognized signs into sentences, and speaks them with the Web Speech API — with a running dashboard and history alongside.

✋

13 word signs

Hello, Stop, Yes, No, Come, Good, Friend, Sorry, Love, Ok, Help, Together, Bye — including two-hand compounds and one motion-tracked sign (Bye).

🔤

16 alphabet letters

A, B, C, D, F, I, J, L, O, S, U, V, W, X, Y, Z fingerspelling, with hold-time confirmation and auto-spacing between words.

🗣

Sentence engine

A phrase-template engine fuses recognized signs (including repeats and 2–3 sign compounds) into a punctuated, spoken sentence.

☁

Firebase history

Detections and finished sentences sync to Realtime Database per browser session, viewable from the History tab.

⌘

Swappable engine

Run entirely on rules, point at your own Flask + TensorFlow backend, or import a TensorFlow.js model straight from a URL.

🌐

Translation preview

A fixed-vocabulary lookup shows Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati and Urdu renderings of recognized sentences alongside the English original.

What's real here: live MediaPipe hand-landmark tracking (21 points/hand, both hands) feeding two independent rule-based geometric classifiers. Word signs mode recognizes 13 whole-word signs — including two-hand compounds (Help, Together) and one motion-tracked sign (Bye, via wrist-trajectory wave detection) — and turns a run of them into a punctuated sentence through a phrase-template engine. Alphabet mode fingerspells 16 ASL letters (A, B, C, D, F, I, J, L, O, S, U, V, W, X, Y, Z) from static handshapes plus two motion-traced letters (J, Z), with a hold-time + rolling-buffer confirmation layer so a shape has to be held steadily before it registers, auto-spacing between words on a short pause, and line finalization into the Conversation log on a longer pause. Both modes share Web Speech API TTS, detection history, CSV export, and a fixed-vocabulary translation table.

What's simplified: both built-in modes are geometry-over-landmarks classifiers, not trained models — they recognize curated, clearly distinct handshapes rather than general ASL/ISL. Alphabet mode only covers 16 of the 26 letters; E, G, H, K, M, N, P, Q, R, and T are left out because they depend on fine thumb/finger overlap or wrist rotation that's unreliable to detect from 2D landmarks with simple geometric rules. J and Z rely on a coarse fingertip-trajectory heuristic, not real motion-sequence modelling, so they're the least reliable letters. The "natural sentence" text in Word signs mode is a hand-written phrase table over 13 known signs, not grammar inference. The new Model & Engine tab lets you swap in a real trained model — either your own Flask + TensorFlow backend, or a TensorFlow.js model imported directly from a URL — for genuinely learned recognition instead of hand-written rules.
